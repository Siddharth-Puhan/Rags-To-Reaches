#RagsToReaches

Sustainable and Locally Sourced Fashion



Rags-To-Reaches is a platform that advocates sustainability and charity by following on the footsteps of Thrift Stores. We receive donations from our loyal customers which is then either put up for sale or donated to charity organisations and NGOs we have partnered with. Fast Fashion has become a leading cause of pollution that is not taken into account by the contemporary generation. We, at Rags-To-Reaches, try to contribute to the cause of reusability by giving purpose to clothes that have been pushed to the deepest of corners of our closets and try to prevent as much waste as we can.

The platform will pose as a common ground of connection between potential customers looking for any of the following services:
     
  1. Artisans with expertise in upholstery sewing like cushions and mattresses.
    2.  
  2. Tailors and seamstresses for custom clothes.
    3.  
  3. Online thrift store for purchasing previously owned clothes.
    4.  
The platform will contain a database of Artisans with expertise in upholstery sewing like cushions and mattresses and contact information along with sample works of Tailors and seamstresses for custom clothes. Any user can signup and visit the portfolios provided and even contact the artisan if they like their work.
